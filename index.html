Player CV Desk

Tell us about a player. Afritech Sports builds a Player CV to our standard and reviews every form before anything is shared with a club, agent or family.

Who is filling in the form?

Player or parentPlayer Scouting & Career Profile FormFor the player, or a parent or guardian when the player is under 18. Covers identity, documents, career, season record, self-assessment, video and consent.Open the player form → Scout, coach or agentPlayer Recruitment & Market Intelligence FormFor scouts, coaches, recruitment analysts and FIFA-licensed agents assessing a player. Covers mandate, evidence, technical, tactical, physical and market assessment.Open the scout and coach form →

How to send it

  1. Fill it in on your phone. Tap a section to open it. Your answers are saved on this phone as you go, so you can close it and come back.
  2. When you are done, tap Review, then Save as PDF.
  3. Email the PDF to Advisory@afritechsports.com.

No account or sign-in needed. Only Afritech Sports sees what you send.
