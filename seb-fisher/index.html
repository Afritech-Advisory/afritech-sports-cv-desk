A 16-year-old Uganda international striker who scored a hat-trick on his debut and won a regional title nine days later. Now named in Uganda's provisional World Cup squad, he is the one to follow through Qatar.

A Scottish-raised Uganda forward with five goals for club and country in 2026, every one off the bench. A development story with a World Cup stage in November and the U-17 Africa Cup of Nations in 2027.

Seb's year in two shirts: Partick Thistle's U-19s in spring, a debut hat-trick and a trophy with Uganda in October, and now a place in the provisional World Cup squad. Here is the plan we see for his next twelve months.
